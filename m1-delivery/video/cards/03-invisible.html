PART 3

The invisible nine-tenths
— built now, visible in later milestones

  • The Institute's complete filing system already exists: applications, referrals, admission decisions, invitations, collaborators, professional profiles with structured CV sections, areas of expertise, organisations, and consent records. Every shelf built before the papers arrive.
  • The admission machinery itself already works and passes its tests: secure one-time invitation links, referral records, and the registration step that turns an invited professional into an admitted collaborator. Milestone 2 gives this machinery its screens.
  • Every email the Portal ever sends will be logged with its delivery status — a bounced invitation can never disappear silently.
  • The permanent history book is already recording — every sign-in today, every admission decision tomorrow.
