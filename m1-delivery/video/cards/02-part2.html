PART 2

How administrative access is protected

  • Signing in as an administrator takes two things: a password, and a six-digit code from a phone.
  • You will now see both: a fully enrolled administrator signing in — and then what happens to an administrator who has not set up their phone code.
  • Every sign-in and every future admission decision is written to a permanent record that no one can erase or edit. Not even the developer.
