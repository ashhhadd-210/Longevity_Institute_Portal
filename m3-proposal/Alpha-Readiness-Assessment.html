1. How close is the Portal to Alpha readiness?

The half of the Portal that admits people is finished. The half they use once they are admitted is not yet built.

To be concrete about what this means today: an Alpha participant would register successfully, sign in, and reach a page that says their profile and the directory open in a forthcoming release. They could recommend a colleague. That is the whole of it.

So the honest answer is that Alpha readiness effectively means completing Milestone 3. That is not a surprise or a change of plan. It is simply where profiles and the directory have always sat.

One release step that is easy to overlook

Everything built in Milestone 2 currently runs on staging. Production is still serving the Milestone 1 release, which is why the application form does not yet answer on the live address. Releasing to production is a deliberate, gated step rather than something that happens automatically, and it is a short piece of work rather than a large one. It has to happen before any real person is invited.

Two small things still outstanding from Milestone 2

5. A realistic route to Alpha

  1. You work through the Field and Specialty questions, at whatever pace suits. This can happen now and costs nothing.
  2. Milestone 3 begins when you are ready. It is three and a half weeks of work.
  3. Within it, you approve the visibility table before the profile is built.
  4. Milestone 2 and 3 are released together to production.
  5. The deliverability tests are completed.
  6. You and I run one complete admission on the live Portal before a single Alpha participant is invited.
  7. Alpha begins.
In other words, Alpha follows Milestone 3 rather than waiting for Milestone 4. Milestone 4 is about hardening, monitoring, documentation, training and the formal launch, which is the right shape of work to do while a small, friendly group is already using the Portal and telling you what they find.
